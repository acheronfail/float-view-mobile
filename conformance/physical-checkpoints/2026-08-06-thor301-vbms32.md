# Attached VBMS32 compatibility: historical result

Historical record revision: [fae1df7c](https://github.com/acheronfail/float-view-mobile/commit/fae1df7cc6673e3f134c2108b152b0fe8920fdff). This identifies the recorded checkpoint, not an independently verified binary hash.

Result: iOS and Android displayed fresh BMS values through a direct controller plus CAN-routed VBMS32. The supported identity is exactly VBMS32, hardware type 2, firmware 6.06; a configured CAN ID is routing state, not product identity.

Limits: Express-gateway and standalone-direct hardware combinations were unavailable. VESC Express 7.00 remains fail-closed pending its own audit/validation.

Detailed device records and execution history are retained locally under
`.local/physical-testing/`. See [recording guidance](README.md) for future checks.

HW_TYPE_CUSTOM_MODULE for VBMS32. `main/hwconf/vesc/vbms32/hw_vbms32.h`
names the hardware; `COMM_BMS_GET_VALUES` routes to `main/bms.c`. The independent
vector is `backends/fixtures/vesc-bms/source-derived.json`, case
`vbms32-firmware-6.06`. Preserve that protocol fixture.
